# NDIC Disclosure Wording (customer-facing)

**Status:** Draft — owner: Compliance Officer, **[COUNSEL]** review before
publication
**Effective:** Phase 9 (Oct 2026)
**Why:** the instinct to say "your money is insured" is where platforms get
into regulatory trouble. PropVest is **not** a bank and is **not** itself
NDIC-insured. The wording below keeps us accurate and still tells customers
the true position of their cash.

---

## 1. The facts we may state

- NDIC insures eligible deposits at **licensed deposit-taking institutions**
  (Deposit Money Banks, Microfinance Banks, PMBs; digital banks are covered
  through their underlying CBN licence, not as a separate category).¹
- Current limits as reported by NDIC: **₦5,000,000 per depositor per DMB**;
  **₦2,000,000** for MFBs/PMBs. A ₦2,000,000 PSB figure is reported but
  **unconfirmed from a primary source — do not publish any figure until
  checked against NDIC directly**.¹
- **Pass-through** insurance can extend to beneficial owners of pool/trust
  accounts, *provided* the account sits at an insured institution and the
  beneficial interests and balances are identifiable and properly documented.
  This must be confirmed with NDIC/the account-holding bank before we claim
  it.¹
- An investment platform (or an investment product) is **not** insured merely
  because it banks with an insured institution or uses a licensed processor.

## 2. Approved wording — wallet/deposit copy

> **Your cash is held at a licensed bank, not with PropVest.** PropVest is an
> investment platform, not a bank, and is not itself licensed by the NDIC.
> Naira balances underlying your PropVest wallet are held in a dedicated
> account with a CBN-licensed institution. Deposit insurance, where it
> applies, is provided by the NDIC to that institution's depositors subject
> to its terms and limits — it is **not** a guarantee of your investments,
> their returns, or any PropVest-issued balance.

Short-form (footer/UI, ≤ 2 lines):

> PropVest is not a bank. NDIC insurance, where applicable, applies to
> eligible deposits at the licensed institution holding your funds — not to
> investments or returns.

## 3. Approved wording — investment risk (place beside any return figure)

> Returns are not guaranteed and are not NDIC-insured. Property values and
> rental income can fall as well as rise. Invest only what you can afford to
> lock up for the holding period.

## 4. Prohibited wording — never publish

- ❌ "NDIC insured" / "your money is insured by the NDIC" (unqualified).
- ❌ "FDIC-style protection", "government guaranteed", "safe as a bank".
- ❌ Any NDIC logo or coverage figure without a current primary-source check.
- ❌ Implying off-plan units or "property shares" are deposits.

## 5. Where the wording must appear

1. Wallet / Add-Funds screen — before the first deposit.
2. Withdrawal screen — beside the amount field.
3. Invest flow — in the mandatory acknowledgment (hold period + terms), next
   to the risk disclosure.
4. `/legal` pages — full version with the NDIC reference.
5. Emails that move money (deposit confirmed, payout sent) — short form.

## 6. Verification checklist before publication

- [ ] Confirm current NDIC per-depositor limits with ndic.gov.ng (primary).
- [ ] Confirm with the account-holding bank whether our account structure

- [ ] Counsel sign-off on §2–§3 wording.

      (search the frontend for "insured", "guaranteed", "safe").
