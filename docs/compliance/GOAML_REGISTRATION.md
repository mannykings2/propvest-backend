# NFIU goAML Portal — Registration & Access Steps

**Status:** Procedure — owner: Compliance Officer
**Why it matters:** the STR/CTR clock (24h / 7 days) only stops when a report
is *filed on goAML* — the platform's Risk panel marks it filed afterwards.
Registration has lead time; **start it before launch, not after the first
report is due.**

---

## 1. What goAML is

The NFIU's (National Financial Intelligence Unit) reporting platform. Nigerian
reporting entities submit Suspicious Transaction Reports (STR), Cash
Transaction Reports (CTR) and other statutory reports through it. Access is
per named user with the reporting entity's registration.

## 2. Registration steps (reporting entity)

1. **Confirm the entity qualifies.** Whether PropVest is itself a reporting
   entity depends on its regulated activity and licence posture (Phase 9
   licence question). Registration is required before the first report is due
   either way — the lead time is the point.
2. **Obtain the goAML onboarding pack** from NFIU (via the state's NFIU office
   or the published onboarding channel). Typical requirements:
   - Certificate of incorporation + CAC documents
   - Regulatory licence / approval (where applicable)
   - Board resolution naming the reporting officer(s)
   - Means of identification for each portal user
   - Contact details of the reporting function
3. **Submit the entity registration** and await credentials/activation.
4. **Register named users** — minimum two (Compliance Officer + a deputy) so a
   single person's absence never breaches the 24-hour clock.
5. **Test the portal** with a dry run before go-live week.

## 3. Operating rules

- **Never share portal credentials.** Each report is attributed to the named
  user who filed it; that attribution is part of the statutory record.
- **Filing is external to the platform.** PropVest's Risk panel tracks the
  obligation (deadline, narrative, reference) — the submission itself happens
  on goAML, and the portal reference is recorded back via *Mark filed*.
- **Deputies must be trained** — see [STAFF_TRAINING.md](STAFF_TRAINING.md).
- If the portal is unavailable near a deadline, file by the NFIU's fallback
  channel and keep the evidence of the attempt; then mark filed with the
  reference obtained.
