# Payment Architecture

## Purpose

The payment subsystem handles deposits, withdrawals, investment funding, and settlement while ensuring financial integrity and reliability.

## Scope

- Payment gateway integration
- Wallet funding
- Withdrawals
- Escrow
- Webhooks
- Reconciliation
- Idempotency
- Settlement
- Failed payments
- Refunds
- Fraud detection

## Principles

- Every payment is traceable.
- Financial operations are idempotent.
- No money is created or destroyed.
- External callbacks are verified.
- Every payment generates immutable records.
